Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to the information you share when you sign up for Because You Can, the book by Divya Jain, on becauseyoucanbook.netlify.app or through a sign-up form on Facebook or Instagram.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, when you choose to give them to us.
  • Your quiz stage (a number from 1 to 8), if you take the quiz and ask for your result. Your individual quiz answers are not saved.
  • Which ad or link brought you to the site, so we know which messages are useful.
  • Anonymous page counts (for example, how many people started or finished the quiz). These are not linked to your name or email.

How we use it

  • To email you your quiz result and one idea to try for your stage.
  • To tell you when first-edition copies of the book are ready, and to share occasional news about the book.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who helps us

Sign-ups are stored securely with Supabase. Emails are sent through Brevo. If you sign up through a Facebook or Instagram form, Meta also processes your details under its own privacy policy. These services only handle your information to provide their service to us.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any email from us. To see, correct or delete your information, write to jain.divya@gmail.com and we will do it promptly.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until the book launch campaign ends or until you ask us to remove them, whichever comes first.
